Frequently Asked Questions

What is HDO Pro?

HDO Pro is a mod of the original HDO Box Android app, built for streaming movies and TV with an ad-free experience and extra enhancements.

What devices are supported?

Android phones, tablets, Android TV, and Fire TV. iOS is no longer supported. HDO Pro may only work on Android 14 and older.

Why do I need a key to download?

Download keys keep the project private and protect the community from abuse. Join the community through the invites page to request a key.

How do I install or update?

Download the APK, open it from your file manager, and allow installation from unknown sources if asked. For updates, uninstall the old version first, then install the new one. The Guide and Video Tutorial cover this in detail.

Is HDO Pro legal?

HDO Pro does not host, upload, store, or distribute any media. Users are responsible for following the laws in their own region. Read the DMCA and Privacy & Safety pages for more.

How do I get support?

Join the community on Discord or Telegram, or use the Support page.

What is the Terminal?

The Terminal is a password-protected download access page for verified members.
