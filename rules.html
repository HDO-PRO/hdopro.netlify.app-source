Server Rules

General Conduct

  • 🔴 NO EXTERNAL PROMOTION
    Do not discuss or promote this server outside of its intended community.
  • 🔴 LANGUAGE
    Communicate in English only. Avoid spamming to keep the environment pleasant for everyone.
  • 🔴 SPOILERS
    Share spoilers only in designated channels.
  • 🔴 RESPECT PRIVACY
    Do not DM or send friend requests to other members without their permission.
  • 🔴 RESPECTFUL BEHAVIOUR
    Avoid racial slurs or derogatory behaviour. Content flagged as derogatory will be dealt with appropriately.
  • 🔴 DISCORD GUIDELINES
    Follow all Discord guidelines.
  • 🔴 SERVER OPERATIONS
    Review the provided information to understand how the server operates.

Content and Posting

  • 🔴 PROMOTIONAL CONTENT
    Do not share promotional content, Discord server invites, torrent links, or discuss external anime streaming sites.

    Share only standard NSFW content in designated NSFW channels. Prohibited content includes child/loli, gore, or animal porn.
  • 🔴 APPROPRIATE CHANNELS
    Post content in the appropriate channels to keep discussions organized.
  • 🔴 NO HARMFUL MATERIAL
    Posting harmful material such as viruses, IP grabbers, or malware will result in an immediate and permanent ban.
  • 🔴 TRADING
    Trading of any sort is strictly prohibited.

Respect and Interaction

  • 🔴 EXCESSIVE LANGUAGE
    Refrain from using excessive extreme language.
  • 🔴 MENTIONS
    Avoid mentioning @everyone, the Moderators, or specific individuals without a valid reason.
  • 🔴 PRIVACY
    Respect others' privacy and refrain from posting personal information without permission.
  • 🔴 INSTRUCTIONS
    Listen to the instructions given by staff members.
  • 🔴 HARASSMENT
    Do not threaten or engage in any form of harassment towards other members.
