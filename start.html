Getting Started

HDO Pro is a private community project. Here's how to get in and get downloading.

  1. Verify you're human. When you arrive you'll get a quick puzzle and a short notice to agree to — this keeps bots and leechers out of the community.
  2. Join the community. Head to the invite page and join us on Discord. That's where invites, updates, and announcements live.
  3. Get the terminal code. Member-only pages ask for a terminal code. It's shared inside the community — ask on Discord if you can't find it. Please don't post it publicly.
  4. Download. Grab builds from the Terminal or the Downloads page. The Guide and Tutorial cover installation.
  5. Optional: install the site. Open the menu and tap "Install" to add HDO Pro to your home screen as an app.

Questions? Check the FAQ or the Support page.
